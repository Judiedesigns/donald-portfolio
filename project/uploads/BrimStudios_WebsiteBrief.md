# **04 — Chosen Design Direction**

## **Confirmed Direction: Minimalist, Work-First**

After evaluating both directions from the original brief, the confirmed approach is the minimalist, work-first direction. This means:

* The work appears immediately — no lengthy introductions or splash screens

* Typography is the primary design system, not decoration

* Restraint is the signal of confidence — nothing on the page is accidental

* Color is used sparingly — the studio’s accent blue earns its appearance

* Animation is present but serves the content, never competes with it

## **What “Minimalist” Means Here**

Minimalism on this site does not mean empty or cold. It means every element has earned its place. The work is rich, the typography is expressive, and the interactions are considered. The minimalism is in the editing — ruthless removal of anything that does not serve the emotional arc.

## **Typography**

Typography is the primary expressive tool on this site. The headline system must be commanding at large scale — the hero headline is a design element as much as it is copy. Consider:

* A high-contrast serif or editorial sans for display headings

* A clean, legible sans for body text and UI labels

* Typographic scale that creates clear hierarchy without relying on color

*The domain tothebrim.studio is itself a typographic and brand asset. The designer should consider how the URL functions as a visual statement — it is a complete sentence that communicates the studio’s philosophy before a single other word is read.*

## **Color**

Working from the brand sprint currently in progress. Confirmed direction:

* Dark background (near-black navy) as the dominant canvas

* One signature blue used as an accent — sparingly and purposefully

* White for primary typography

The blue must earn every appearance on screen. It is not a brand color that floods the page — it is a punctuation mark. Column one of the brand sprint palette options is the strongest foundation.

## **Animation & Interaction Principles**

* Scroll-triggered entrances: subtle, not theatrical. Confidence through restraint.

* Project visuals: parallax or horizontal scroll movement as user scrolls through

* NDA project hover state: subtle blur or redaction animation on hover

* CTA section: background shift (dark to light or inverse) to signal arrival at a decision point

* No loading animations that overstay their welcome

* No decorative motion that competes with content

# **06 — Video Asset Brief**

Three video assets are to be produced by Donald for use across the site. All video plays silently, autoplays, loops. No controls visible. All files must be compressed below 5MB per clip before uploading to Framer.

## **Video 01 — Detail Reel**

**USED IN**

Hero section — ambient background behind the headline

**WHAT IT IS**

A 15–20 second reel of close-cropped UI details from both live projects. Not full-page views — fragments. A button hover state. A heading animating in. A card lifting on scroll. A cursor interacting with a navigation element. Dark Visionairy detail alternates with light CFMS detail. The contrast in close-up makes the range feel intentional.

**MOOD**

Craft-focused. Obsessive. This is the “we care about details” video.

**TREATMENT ON SITE**

Plays at reduced opacity behind the headline. Copy sits on top, fully readable. Motion underneath suggests depth without competing.

## **Video 02 — Scroll Journey**

**USED IN**

Visionairy project section

**WHAT IT IS**

A clean 8–12 second screen recording of someone scrolling through the Visionairy site. No visible cursor. Scroll speed is slow — every animation trigger lands. The loop point is a particularly striking transition or animation moment.

**MOOD**

Deliberate. Technical. Premium.

**CAPTURE TOOLS**

Screenstudio (Mac) or OBS for raw capture. Rotato or Screenstudio for device framing if needed. Compress with Handbrake.

## **Video 03 — Build Process**

**USED IN**

Studio section — alongside or behind founder photo

**WHAT IT IS**

A time-lapse or sped-up (4–8x) screen recording of work in Framer. The canvas is visible — components being placed, interactions being wired, the canvas zooming. Cropped to Framer canvas only. Duration on screen: 10–15 seconds looped.

**MOOD**

Human. Process-driven. “You can see how this gets made.”

# **07 — Founder Photo Brief**

One or two photos of Donald to be used in the Studio section (primary) and optionally near the CTA (secondary, warmer expression). Self-shot. The photo must communicate competence and approachability simultaneously.

## **Setup**

* Natural light from a side window at 45 degrees to the face

* Clean wall background — white, off-white, or dark

* Phone mounted at eye level on a stable surface

* Rear camera only — not front-facing

* 10-second timer with burst mode, minimum 30–40 frames captured

* Dark, solid-color clothing — no logos, no busy patterns

## **Framing & Expression**

* Chest-up framing — not a tight face crop

* Look slightly off-camera (preferred) or directly into lens

* Expression: controlled, slight smile — competent and interested, not eager or cold

* Think: the founding partner of a studio you’ve heard good things about

## **Post-Processing**

Run through Lightroom Mobile (free). Three adjustments only: exposure up slightly if dark, a touch of contrast, desaturate background slightly if competing. No filters. No heavy editing. The goal is a better version of reality, not a processed photo.

## **Usage on Site**

| Studio Section | Alongside or behind studio copy. Composed, slightly serious. Statement of presence. |
| :---- | :---- |
| **CTA Section (optional)** | Small, incidental crop near the booking CTA. Slightly warmer expression. Human face before the decision moment. |
